# English reading guide

This guide describes the retained Chinese documents so readers can locate relevant material. It is a summary, not a full translation or verification of the source equations and claims.

## Modeling research

`source/建模研究.docx` contains notes on geometric configuration, the relationship between strut length and spacing, cable length, node coordinates for two-point ground contact, Euler angles for triangular ground contact, and transition-distance parameters. It then introduces form finding using the force-density method, strut and cable connectivity matrices, and Newton–Euler dynamics through quaternions, rotation matrices, and angular-velocity coordinate transformations. Some mathematical material is embedded as graphics. Consult the original document for the equations; values and units have not been independently verified.

## Ultrasonic cleaning concept

`source/超声波清淤设计.docx` explores cleaning through ultrasonic excitation and cavitation. It identifies a transducer and ultrasonic power supply, discusses potential cleaning applications, includes preliminary component-selection links, and notes that frequency should depend on the operating environment and target. This is an exploratory design note, not test evidence or an implemented subsystem.

## Motion illustrations

`source/张拉运动示意图/` contains six JPEG illustrations and a Word document with six embedded images. The Word document has no substantive extracted text; it has been retained as an image document, not treated as empty.

## Internal components

`source/内部元件及说明/` contains PDF drawings named for a hemisphere cover, retaining ring and flange, a component-list PDF, and STEP models of a controller, motor controller, geared motor and battery assembly. This guide identifies their role from filenames; it is not a translation of the PDF labels or a verification of their specifications.
